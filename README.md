# Welcome to StonksPad

**StonksPad is a token launchpad on BNB Chain where every token pays its holders in tokenized stocks.**

Launch a token in one click, pick the stocks it should reward its holders with (Nvidia, Tesla, SpaceX, the Nasdaq-100 and more), and let the trading taxes do the rest. Creators earn fees, holders earn stocks, and the platform's own share buys and burns **$STONKS**.

StonksPad runs on [Flap](https://flap.sh) infrastructure, the largest launchpad on BNB Chain, through a custom, open Flap Tax Vault. Your token is a normal Flap token; StonksPad adds the stock-reward engine on top.

## In one minute

| Who | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | Launches a token, chooses stock pairs, decides how trading fees are split, and routes their share to a wallet or an X handle. |
| **Holders** | Receive a share of the token's stock rewards, proportional to their balance, claimable from the site. |
| **StonksPad** | Takes a fixed cut of trading fees, 80% of which buys and burns $STONKS. |
