# FAQ

**Is StonksPad its own chain or DEX?**
No. It runs on BNB Chain and uses Flap for token creation and bonding curves, and PancakeSwap for trading after graduation and for buying stocks.

**Do I need an X account?**
No. You can use your own wallet. X login is optional and gives you a managed wallet, which is also how creators can pay fees to X handles.

**I received tokens or fees "on my X handle". Where are they?**
Sign in to stonkspad.sh with that X account. The wallet that was created for your handle becomes yours, with everything in it.

**Why do I wait 30 minutes (or 24 hours) to claim stocks?**
Distributions are computed off-chain and independently verified. 30 minutes after verification is the normal path; 24 hours is the fallback that protects holders if verification fails.

**Can the team take my rewards?**
No. Rewards sit in the vault until you claim them; there is no withdrawal function for the operator. See [Security](security.md).

**What are the stock tokens exactly?**
Tokenized stocks issued on BNB Chain (currently Binance's bStocks). They are regular BEP-20 tokens tracking the underlying share price.

**Can I add my own asset as a pair?**
Not yet. Assets are added by StonksPad after a liquidity and price-source check. Suggestions are welcome on X.

**What happens after graduation?**
The token moves to PancakeSwap V2 with locked liquidity. Taxes, vaults, rewards and claims keep working exactly as before.

**What does the platform earn?**
10% of trading taxes, of which 80% is used to buy and burn $STONKS. See [Fees](fees.md).

**Is the code open?**
Yes. Every contract is verified on BscScan; the vault follows Flap's public vault specification.
